Title: AJR Dev Log v2.3

Matte-black restyle, Projects section and a new icon.

- Restyled with a matte black base, fine film grain, a faint grid behind the hero, mono HUD-style labels, square corners and corner brackets. The ice and ember accents stay.
- Added a short, rare glitch on the hero title (off for visitors who prefer reduced motion).
- Added a Projects tab and a section with three "coming soon" cards.
- New icon set (SVG, 96x96 PNG, Apple touch icon, favicon.ico) with a glitch-sliced "A" mark, also shown beside the name in the header.
- New social preview image to match, plus twitter:image and twitter:image:alt tags.
- Header fixed on narrow phones: no overflow down to 320 px.
