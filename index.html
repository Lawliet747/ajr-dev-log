About me

Hi! I'm Anthony Joseph Robles, an aspiring web developer from Caloocan, Metro Manila. I completed my BS in Information Technology at the University of the East on an IT generalist track; covering programming, networking, web development and database administration.

My original plan was to program video games or animate for a studio like Pixar. Then, I took my first web development course. After this, game algorithms and endless animation hours sounded draining. Meanwhile, building websites gave me energy instead.

After graduating in 2021, I started my career in tech support and spent nearly five years as a Support Analyst and Associate Consultant. I supported enterprise applications, resolved incidents and service requests, and worked with ServiceNow, JIRA, Confluence, PeopleSoft and Microsoft Dynamics 365. That work sharpened my troubleshooting, documentation and client communication. I'm now looking for roles where I can use my tech support background or apply my web development skills.

I'm always learning on my own time, training myself in other areas of IT because knowledge is power. Away from the keyboard, you'll find me watching movies and series, or listening to music.

"Excellence is the gradual result of always striving to do better."

-Pat Riley-
